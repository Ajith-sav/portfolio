## 3. Private Owner Admin CMS (All 8 Modules Explained)

Once unlocked via your secret shortcut and passcode, your private Admin CMS provides **8 dedicated modules**:

### Module 1 — Profile, Contact & Hero Metrics
* Edit your full name, target role headline, location, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, hero status badge, and current role card title.
* Customize both the **values** (`3+ Yrs`, `99%`, `6+`, `8.0`) and the **labels** (`Production Full Stack`, `AI Classifier Accuracy`, `Enterprise Platforms`, `B.E. CSE CGPA`) for all 4 hero metric counters.
* Edit your **Professional Summary** (used on your downloadable PDF resume).

### Module 2 — Landing Page Content Customization
* **Hero & Navbar Copy**: Customize your navbar role tag (`FULL STACK & AI ENGINEER`), your 3-part Hero Main Heading (*Start Text*, *Animated Gradient Highlight Text*, and *End Text*), and your Hero Landing Bio paragraph.
* **System Verification Card**: Customize your current role domain subtitle, comma-separated Multi-Database Architecture badges (`PostgreSQL, AWS Redshift, AWS HBase, MySQL, Redis / Valkey`), and availability status text (`Immediate Impact Ready`).
* **"Why HR & Engineering Leaders Hire Me" (Recruiter Pitch Section)**:
  * Edit the section eyebrow tag, main heading, and subtitle description.
  * **Full CRUD & Reordering for Pitch Cards**: Add new pitch cards, edit card titles/icons/accent colors/descriptions, reorder cards with `↑` / `↓`, or delete cards.
* **Section Headings & Contact CTA**: Customize the headings for Skills, Experience, Projects, Education, and the Contact section CTA.

### Module 3 — Technical Skills CRUD & Reordering
* Add new skill categories, edit comma-separated skills, reorder categories up/down (`↑` / `↓`), or remove categories.

### Module 4 — Work Experience CRUD & Reordering
* Edit your roles at **Switch Mobility EV**, **GI Scripts**, and **Trillion Thoughts Technologies**, reorder roles (`↑ Move Up` / `↓ Move Down`), or click **Add New Role / Company** when you get promoted or switch companies in the future.

### Module 5 — Production Projects Manager & Card Order Arrangement
* **Live Visual Order Strip**: See a numbered strip at the top of Module 5 showing the exact display sequence of your project cards (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`...) with quick `←` / `→` arrows.
* **4 Ways to Arrange Project Card Order**:
  1. **Move Up (`↑ Up`) / Move Down (`↓ Down`)** buttons on every project card.
  2. **Pin #1 (`Pin #1`)** button to immediately promote any flagship project to the first spot on both your landing page and your PDF resume.
  3. **Position Selector Dropdown (`Position #1` – `Position #N`)** to jump any project card directly to a specific slot.
  4. **Quick Strip Arrows (`←` / `→`)** at the top of Module 5.
* Edit project titles, highlight badges, filter categories (`ai`, `telematics`, `security`), tech stacks, footer left/right tags, and achievement bullets.

### Module 6 — Education & Academic Capstones CRUD
* Update degree details, graduation year, CGPA, and manage or reorder your college capstone projects (*Brain Tumor Detection using MRI Scan Report* and *Car Resale Value Prediction*).

### Module 7 — Preview CV & Dynamic PDF Studio
* **Preview CV (A4 Sheet)**: Inspect your live A4 resume layout, switch between **Role-Tailored Presets** (*Full Stack & Telematics*, *Applied AI & FastAPI Focus*, *React.js & Frontend Focus*), or toggle whether your college capstone projects appear on the PDF.
* **Dual PDF Engine**:
  * **Mode A (Default — Auto-Generated Vector PDF)**: Uses the `jsPDF` vector engine to build a multi-page, ATS-readable `Ajith_E_Resume_2026.pdf` directly from your live Admin data (respecting your exact project card order).
  * **Mode B (Custom Uploaded PDF Override)**: Lets you upload any custom `.pdf` file from your computer so all public **Download Resume** buttons serve that exact file.

### Module 8 — Permanent Export & Security PIN
* **1. Export Updated `index.html`**: Downloads a brand-new, single-file `index.html` with all of your latest Admin edits, landing page customizations, and project card order permanently baked into the source code.
* **2. Backup Data (`.JSON`) / 3. Restore from `.JSON`**: Export or import a lightweight JSON backup of your entire portfolio state.
* **Change Secret Owner Passcode**: Update your login PIN from `2026` to any custom passcode.

## 4. How to Customize, Reorder & Publish Updates Free

1. **Unlock Admin CMS**: Open your portfolio and press **`Ctrl + Shift + A`** (or tap the **`AE` logo 5 times** on mobile). Enter your passcode (`2026`).
2. **Make Your Edits**:
   * Update landing page copy or pitch cards in **Module 2**.
   * Add new roles or skills in **Modules 3 & 4**.
   * Arrange project card order or add new projects in **Module 5**.
   * Preview your formatted A4 resume in **Module 7**.
3. **Save Live**: Click **Save & Apply All Live**.
4. **Export Permanent File**: Go to **Module 8 (Export & Security PIN)** and click **1. Export Updated `index.html`**.
5. **Deploy Free in 30 Seconds**:
   * **Netlify Drop**: Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) and drag the folder containing your newly exported `index.html` into the browser window.
   * **GitHub Pages / Vercel**: Commit and push your updated `index.html` to your repository to update your live link worldwide.
